  • Bernhard Schirm presented "HELM in Action: Complex oligonucleotides"


2018 Activities

ACS National Meeting 19 August Boston

  • Sergio Rotstein presented "HELM: Continuing to set the standard for biomolecular representation "

  • Yohann Potier presented "Registering chemically modified oligonucleotides: Implementations and challenges "
  • Markus Weisser presented "Similarity analysis of oligonucleotides based on HELM notation"
  • Jeff Milton presented "monomer.org: The global hub for (bio) polymer informatics. "
  • Noel O'Boyle presented "Building a bridge between human-readable and machine-readable representations of biopolymers."


Pistoia Alliance European Meeting 14 March London

    • HELM poster and a short presentation from Sergio Rotstein.

ACS 20-22nd August Washington DC

  • Matthias Nolte presented "HELM: An open standard for biomolecule structure representation and exchange"
  • Perkin Elmer announced their adoption of HELM in ChemDraw.

    • Claire Bellamy presented "HELM Setting the standard for biomolecular exchange"

HELM Software for Biopolymers, Jeff Milton et al, Journal of Chemical Information and Modelling, 2017. 57(6), 123-1239

ACS San Francisco 2nd-6th April 2017

    • Jeff Milton presented " HELM: A line notation and software infrastructure for managing biopolymers"

InChI Meeting at the EMBL-EBI  20-21st March 2017

    • Claire Bellamy presented "Why large molecule standards are important to pharma" in the main session.
    • Markus Weisser presented "Representation of ambiguous biomolecules using the Enhanced Standard HELM 2.0" in the large molecules group.

Press release 18th January 2017

    • HELM web-editor version 1 release announcement
